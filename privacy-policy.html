Privacy Policy

TLS Tec · Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RDK (Pvt) Ltd. (“RDK”, “we”, “us”) handles information in the TLS Tec mobile application (“TLS Tec” or “the App”). TLS Tec helps TLS Technology manage products, quotations, invoices, payments, PDF documents, and database backups.

Information stored in the App

The information you enter or create in TLS Tec may include:

  • Customer names, phone numbers, installation addresses, invoice numbers and dates.
  • Products, categories, item descriptions, serial numbers, warranty details, quantities, prices, discounts, and payment records.
  • Your business name, address, phone numbers, email address, invoice prefix, and invoice terms.
  • App preferences and backup status, such as backup frequency and the time of the last backup.

This information is stored in the App’s local SQLite database in the App’s private storage on your device. RDK does not operate a TLS Tec server that receives or stores these records.

Google Sign-In and Google Drive backups

If you choose to connect a Google account, TLS Tec receives the Google account email address needed to show which account is connected. The App requests the Google Drive drive.appdata permission so it can save and restore TLS Tec backup files in the App’s private Google Drive application data folder.

A backup can contain the information stored in your local TLS Tec database, including customer, invoice, product, payment, and business settings. A Drive backup is uploaded only when you request a backup or enable automatic backups. Restore downloads the backup from the Google account currently connected in the App.

TLS Tec uses Google user data only to provide the sign-in, backup, and restore features you request. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it for profiling. RDK does not receive a copy of your Drive backup. Google handles account authentication and Drive storage under Google’s own terms and privacy practices. See Google’s Privacy Policy and the Google API Services User Data Policy.

PDFs, backups, and sharing

You can create invoice or quotation PDFs and share them using the Android sharing menu. You may also choose to share a database backup file. When you select another app or recipient, the information in that PDF or backup is sent to the app or recipient you selected and is then handled under their terms and privacy policy. Please check the recipient before sharing.

When information is shared

RDK does not sell or rent information stored in TLS Tec. Information leaves your device only when you choose an App feature that sends it to Google Drive, or when you choose to share a PDF or backup with another app or person. Google Sign-In and Google Drive process the information needed to provide those features.

Retention and your choices

  • You can disable automatic backups in Settings. You can disconnect TLS Tec from your Google Account in your Google Account security settings; this stops future access until you connect again.
  • Local records remain on your device until you delete them in the App, clear the App’s storage, or uninstall the App. Clearing storage or uninstalling can permanently remove local records, so save a backup first if you need one.
  • Google Drive backups remain associated with the Google Account used for the backup until they are replaced or removed through the available Google account/app data controls. Revoking access may not itself delete an existing backup.
  • PDFs and backup files shared to another app may remain with that app or recipient. Manage or request deletion from the service or person who received them.

Security

TLS Tec stores its database in the device’s private App storage and communicates with Google Drive over Google’s APIs. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Protect your device and Google Account, and avoid sharing backup files with people you do not trust.

Children’s privacy

TLS Tec is a business utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly design the App to collect children’s information.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the App’s features or data practices change. The current version and its update date will be published at this page.
